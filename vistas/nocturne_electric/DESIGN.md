## Brand & Style

The design system is crafted for high-energy live event discovery, fast ticket purchasing, and autonomous event management on mobile devices. Designed specifically for music festivals, stage performances, nightlife, and cultural events, it evokes excitement, anticipation, and digital sophistication.

### Personality & Emotional Response
- **Vibrant & Immersive:** Transports users into the concert or festival atmosphere before they even arrive at the venue.
- **Electric & Kinetic:** Pulsing accents create strong focal clarity for checkout pathways, live updates, and urgent countdowns.
- **Trustworthy & Modern:** Crisp glassmorphism surfaces paired with deep, midnight violet canvases provide readability and frictionless monetary transactions.

### Design Movement
- **Glassmorphism & Neon Tonal Layering:** Deep indigo and midnight-violet surfaces layered with translucent containers (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` to `rgba(123, 44, 191, 0.15)`), delicate neon highlights, and high-impact magenta/fuchsia call-to-actions.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for pure dark-mode immersion, rooted in the energy of night concerts, stages, and neon lighting.

### Functional Roles
- **Primary (`#E01A8A` / `#FF007F`):** Dedicated to high-conversion conversion triggers, primary purchase buttons ("COMPRAR ENTRADAS"), instant checkout steps, and active tabs.
- **Secondary (`#7B2CBF` / `#3B1E6D`):** Structural chromatic foundation. Governs headers, glass surface borders, elevated card backgrounds, and category pill selections.
- **Tertiary (`#00D2F2`):** Informational neon cyan. Expresses active inventory ("Disponible"), ticket counts, real-time indicators, and event badge states.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#0F081D` & `#180D36`):** Provides an ultra-dark indigo/violet backdrop that eliminates visual glare, boosts contrast for event photography, and makes vibrant accents glow naturally.

## Typography

The typography leverages `Plus Jakarta Sans`, balancing modern geometric precision with friendly humanistic curves. Its balanced proportions guarantee legible ticket data, prices, countdowns, and organizer metadata on small mobile screens.

- **Headlines & Titles:** Set in bold weights with subtle negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to command attention for artist and event names.
- **Labels & Call-to-Actions:** Set in uppercase or capitalized semi-bold/bold with expanded tracking (`0.02em` to `0.05em`) to ensure quick recognition in dimly lit or on-the-go scenarios.
- **Numbers & Monetary Values:** Utilize tabular numeric alignments when showing ticket tiers (e.g. `General $15.000 / VIP $30.000`) for instantaneous scanning.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for fluid touch interactions, optimized for mobile thumb zones and single-hand navigation in React Native.

### Layout Rhythm
- **Canvas Margins:** Fixed `1.25rem` (20px) horizontal screen padding ensures full touch accessibility and prevents UI clipping near device bezels.
- **Vertical Spacing:** Modules adhere to an 8pt rhythmic grid (`0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2rem`) to structure event hero images, ticket tiers, chat bubbles, and transaction summaries cleanly.
- **Sticky Purchase Bar:** The primary purchase trigger remains anchored to the bottom sheet safe-area with `1rem` vertical padding and translucent background blur for seamless bottom-up navigation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through translucent glassmorphic surfaces, layered dark containers, and subtle colored neon halos rather than traditional heavy black drop shadows.

- **Base Layer (Level 0):** Pure background canvas `#0F081D`.
- **Card Surfaces (Level 1):** `#180D36` with a `1px` outer stroke of `rgba(123, 44, 191, 0.35)` and a subtle ambient glow `0px 8px 24px rgba(15, 8, 29, 0.7)`.
- **Active / Interactive Elements (Level 2):** `#22134D` or floating glass overlays with backdrop blur (blur radius 16px to 24px) paired with an inner top border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`.
- **Primary CTAs & Glows (Level 3):** Fuchsia/Magenta buttons emit a soft diffused neon aura `0px 6px 20px rgba(224, 26, 138, 0.45)` to guide immediate user interaction.

## Shapes

The design uses friendly, rounded corners to contrast with the dark background.

- **Primary Cards & Modals:** Standardized on `rounded-2xl` (16px / 1rem) for an ergonomic, card-deck mobile feel.
- **Interactive Buttons & Badges:** Use generous pill radii (`9999px` or full roundedness) for primary buttons, category tags, and status indicators.
- **Input Fields & Chat Bubbles:** Rounded at `12px` to `16px` with custom corner pinning for chat messages.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("COMPRAR ENTRADAS"):** Solid `#E01A8A` or gradient `#FF007F` to `#E01A8A`, full width, uppercase bold typography (`label-lg`), height `52px`, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), with a subtle magenta glow.
- **Secondary / Action Outlines:** Background `#180D36`, border `1.5px solid #7B2CBF`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Floating Heart / Bookmark Button:** Circular glass container (`rgba(24, 13, 54, 0.65)`), border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`, icon color `#FFFFFF` or `#FF007F` when active.

### Cards & Ticket Tiers
- **Event Hero Card:** High-bleed 16:9 media container, overlay gradient from transparent to `#180D36`, rounded bottom corners.
- **Ticket Selection Card:** Surface `#180D36`, subtle violet border (`#7B2CBF` at 30% opacity), internal padding `1rem`. Clearly displays tier label, pricing in tabular bold text, and availability status.
- **Glass Panel:** `rgba(24, 13, 54, 0.8)` with backdrop blur, giving depth without obscuring rich photography beneath.

### Badges & Chips
- **Availability Badge ("Disponible"):** Pill shape, dark cyan tint background (`rgba(0, 210, 242, 0.12)`), neon cyan dot indicator, text `#00D2F2` (`label-sm`).
- **Category Chips:** Horizontal scrolling pills, unselected `#180D36` with violet border, selected `#7B2CBF` with white text.

### Inputs & Chat
- **Search Bar:** Pill-shaped input container, background `#180D36`, border `1px solid rgba(123, 44, 191, 0.4)`, placeholder text `#8A7EA6`, leading search icon.
- **Transaction Chat Bubbles:**
  - *Organizer/System:* Background `#22134D`, left-aligned, border `1px solid rgba(123, 44, 191, 0.3)`.
  - *Buyer:* Background `#7B2CBF`, right-aligned, text `#FFFFFF`.

### Navigation
- **Floating Bottom Tab Bar:** Dark glass bar (`rgba(15, 8, 29, 0.92)`), upper border `1px solid rgba(123, 44, 191, 0.25)`, icons in `#8A7EA6`, active tab illuminated in `#00D2F2` or `#E01A8A` with an ambient glow indicator.
